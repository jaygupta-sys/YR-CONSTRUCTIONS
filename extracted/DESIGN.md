## Brand & Style

This design system articulates the pedigree, precision, and permanence of an elite architectural and construction practice. Inspired by collectible architectural monographs, museum catalogs, and haute horlogerie print literature, the UI treats the screen as archival paper and patinated stone rather than disposable digital canvas.

### Target Audience & Persona
The experience is tailored to ultra-high-net-worth individuals, institutional patrons, contemporary art collectors, and architectural prize committees. The interface must communicate unquestioned authority, tactile craftsmanship, and understated quiet luxury—avoiding flashiness, rapid animations, or generic SaaS aesthetics.

### Design Aesthetics & Philosophy
- **Editorial Archivalism:** Alternating heavy structural charcoal foundations with luminous warm ivory plates, mimicking the deliberate physical pacing of opening a bespoke linen-bound monograph.
- **Asymmetric Composition & Structural Grid:** Unbalanced, deliberate columns, expansive white/dark space, architectural datum lines, and rigorous typographic hierarchies.
- **Subtle Tactility:** Low-contrast hairline borders, brushed champagne metallic accents, and refined micro-typography reminiscent of engraved brass architectural tags.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising rhythm of material weight. Dark sections feel grounded in basalt and charred timber; light sections evoke porous travertine and raw linen paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Charcoal (`#111210`):** The foundational tone of mass, volume, and shadow. Used as the default canvas for primary project displays, master narratives, and deep structural footers.
- **Secondary Warm Ivory (`#F3F0E8`):** A natural limestone surface. Used for inverted editorial sections, essay panels, material spec sheets, and high-readability architectural critiques.
- **Tertiary Muted Champagne Gold (`#B69A70`):** An alloy accent inspired by brushed architectural hardware, bronze window mullions, and gilded spine stamping. Reserved strictly for active indicators, refined micro-badges, and focal points.
- **Neutral Warm Slate Grey (`#99968E`):** The functional intermediary for technical metadata, structural hairline rules, drawing notations, and secondary captions.
- **Off-White Accent (`#F5F3EE`):** Luminous archival text color ensuring effortless contrast across charcoal surfaces without the stark harshness of pure `#FFFFFF`.

### Editorial Section Contrast
Sections intentionally alter base tones: full-bleed `#111210` sequences transition cleanly into full-bleed `#F3F0E8` plates with reciprocal text colors (`#F5F3EE` on dark; `#111210` on light).

## Typography

Typography acts as the primary architectural element of the layout. The pairing contrasts the historic lyricism of high-contrast display serifs with the calm, clinical discipline of geometric sans-serif micro-typography.

### Editorial Hierarchy
- **Headline Expression (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for monumental project titles, master manifesto statements, and structural indices. Set in lighter weights (`400`–`500`) with tight leading to simulate traditional book jacket typography.
- **Body & Micro Typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and ultra-legible. Body copy uses a light weight (`300`) with generous leading to convey space and serenity.
- **Architectural Notation (`label-caps`):** Rendered in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.22em`), evoking index codes, construction project codes, site elevations, and blueprint legends.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces expansive pacing and intentional asymmetry. Space is not passive void; it is the structural medium through which photography and architectural forms breathe.

### Grid & Composition
- **12-Column Asymmetric Architectural Grid:** Content conforms to 12 irregular pairings (e.g., an 8-column heroic bleed image offset against a 3-column project brief with a 1-column structural spacer).
- **Datum Lines:** Sections are punctuated by razor-thin continuous rules spanning the layout, anchoring metadata to image edges like precision dimension lines on CAD drawings.
- **Scale Shifts:** Spacing jumps dramatically from intimate micro-gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) inside project specifications to monumental programmatic separations (`space-xl`) between distinct architectural case studies.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Full 12-column grid with generous 4rem canvas margins and dramatic horizontal offsets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout; gutter shifts to 2rem; margin compressed to 2.5rem. Image/text pairings fold into rhythmic alternating blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked monograph format; gutters collapse to 1.25rem; margins to 1.5rem. Wide whitespace is preserved vertically to retain luxury pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic drop shadows, floating cards, and artificial elevation layers. Physical presence is achieved through planar layering, razor-sharp hairline borders, and pure tonal contrast.

### Planar Tiers & Rules
- **Ghost Rules & Datum Borders:** Structural divisions use 1px solid borders set at 15% opacity (`rgba(243, 240, 232, 0.15)` on dark backgrounds; `rgba(17, 18, 16, 0.12)` on warm ivory).
- **Tonal Overlays:** Image panels sit flush or overlap other structural planes without cast shadows, relying on subtle tone shifts (`#171916` over `#111210`) to establish spatial foreground and background.
- **Glass Transparency:** For floating overlays (e.g., persistent project navigators or image lightbox controls), use a heavy, ultra-refined frosted layer: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` with an 85% opacity surface and a 1px perimeter stroke tinted in muted champagne (`rgba(182, 154, 112, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

In keeping with architectural principles of precision masonry, structural steel, and monolith slabs, geometry is purely rectilinear (`roundedness: 0`).

- **Corners:** Absolutely sharp (0px border-radius) across every element—including cards, interactive buttons, modal frames, inputs, and image frames.
- **Architectural Framing:** Imagery is treated as physical architectural apertures. Cut lines are crisp, clean, and unsoftened.
- **Circular Exceptions:** The sole exception to the razor grid is the interactive bespoke cursor or circular architectural compass indicator, acting as a singular lens or surveyor's mark against absolute rectangular geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Architectural CTA:** Sharp rectangular frame with 1px border. Dark canvas variant: `#111210` fill with 1px `#B69A70` border and `#F5F3EE` text in `label-caps`. Hover activates a slow, champagne gold interior flood (`#B69A70`) with `#111210` text.
- **Textual Anchor Link:** Monospaced or tracked sans label with an extended horizontal baseline rule that extends +16px beyond the text edge, retracting slightly on hover.

### Architectural Portfolio Cards
- **Structure:** Zero-radius container with no outer drop shadow. Full-width photographic frame bounded by a 1px structural hairline.
- **Metadata Tagging:** Project elevation, year of completion, and site coordinates placed beneath the frame in `label-caps` and `label-mono-num` using `#99968E`.
- **Title Block:** Project name in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`), transitioning to italicized state on subtle hover.

### Form Inputs & Inquiry Fields
- **Understated Line Fields:** Borderless top, left, and right; single bottom border (1px hairline in `#99968E` at 30% opacity). Active state shifts bottom rule to `#B69A70` (100%).
- **Typography:** Floating placeholder labels using `label-caps` in `#99968E`. Input text appears in `body-md` in `#F5F3EE` or `#111210`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Geometry:** Strict geometric squares (0px radius) for checkboxes; minimal hairline rings for radio markers.
- **Active State:** Inner fill consists of a solitary solid geometric square in `#B69A70` recessed by 2px internal padding.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Index List / Monograph Table of Contents:** Full-width editorial list with project index numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `label-mono-num`, project name in `headline-md`, location, and typology. Separated by continuous horizontal hairline rules that illuminate to `#B69A70` on row hover.
- **Materiality & Specimen Chip:** Rectangular stone or wood veneer preview swatch framed in a 1px border with adjacent architectural nomenclature in `body-sm`.
- **Architectural Drawing Viewer:** High-contrast inverted blueprint panel featuring fine lines, zoom viewport controls, and champagne coordinate markers.
